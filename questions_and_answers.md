# Speaking Assessment Modules and Submodules Question Bank

This document outlines the speaking assessment questions, context, and grading tips grouped by the corresponding learning submodules for each proficiency level.

---

## 1. BEGINNER LEVEL: Pronunciation Basics (Read-Aloud Tasks)
Mastering the fundamental sounds of English—vowels, consonants, word stress, and sentence intonation.

### Submodule 1: Vowel Sounds
Focused on clear articulation of long/short vowels and diphthongs.

#### Task B-V1. Long and Short Vowels (/u:/ and /o:/)
*   **Passage to Read:** "The blue coat is too warm for the hot room."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Focus on the distinction between the long /u:/ sound in "blue" / "room" and the /o:/ sounds in "coat".
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-V2. Word Contrast (/ɪ/ and /i:/)
*   **Passage to Read:** "He lives in a big house near the center of the city."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Focus on the short /ɪ/ sound in "lives", "big", and "city" versus the long /i:/ sound in "near".
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

---

### Submodule 2: Consonant Sounds
Focused on clear articulation of complex consonant clusters and sibilants.

#### Task B-C1. Sibilants (/s/ and /ʃ/)
*   **Passage to Read:** "She sells seashells by the seashore."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Clearly differentiate between the unvoiced sibilant /s/ in "sells"/"seashells" and the fricative /ʃ/ in "she"/"seashore".
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-C2. Full Consonant Range
*   **Passage to Read:** "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Articulate all consonants clearly, paying special attention to the voiced 'v', 'z', and the 'th' sound in "the".
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

---

### Submodule 3: Word Stress
Focused on placing emphasis on the correct syllables for nouns vs. verbs and key content words.

#### Task B-S1. Noun vs. Verb Stress
*   **Passage to Read:** "You must record the details in the database before you play back the recording."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Stress the first syllable for nouns ("RE-cord") and the second syllable for verbs ("re-CORD").
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-S2. Content Word Emphasis
*   **Passage to Read:** "Let's focus on the customer's main concern first."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Emphasize the primary noun "customer" and the action verb "focus" to convey meaning clearly.
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

---

### Submodule 4: Sentence Intonation
Focused on correct vocal pitch modulation at the end of statements, yes/no questions, and open-ended queries.

#### Task B-I1. Yes/No Question Intonation (Rising)
*   **Passage to Read:** "Are you joining us today, or are you staying here?"
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Use rising intonation at the end of the choice question to sound naturally inquiring.
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-I2. WH-Question Intonation (Falling)
*   **Passage to Read:** "Where is the customer billing info listed on your screen?"
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Use falling intonation at the end of this WH-question ("Where") to project professional confidence.
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-I3. Verification Questions (Rising)
*   **Passage to Read:** "Could you repeat that last digit of your phone number?"
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Use rising intonation for polite verification questions to sound customer-oriented.
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

#### Task B-I4. Polite Introductions (Steady/Friendly)
*   **Passage to Read:** "Good morning! Welcome to customer support. My name is Sarah."
*   **Pronunciation Tip:** Maintain a warm, friendly tone and steady pace with positive emphasis on greetings.
*   **Passing Score:** 70% Speech-to-Text F1 Similarity

## 2. INTERMEDIATE LEVEL: BPO Call Handling (Scenario Roleplay)
Focused on active listening, problem-solving, empathy, and professional communication in call scenarios.

### Submodule 1: Professional Call Opening & Verification
*   **Scenario Context:** A customer calls about their monthly invoice. They sound slightly confused.
*   **AI Customer Opening Message:** *"Hi, I just received my bill and something doesn't look right."*
*   **Evaluation Focus:** Professional greeting, validating the customer's intent, and politely verifying identity before proceeding.
*   **Coaching Tip:** Greet professionally, verify the caller's identity before pulling up the account.

### Submodule 2: Active Listening & Paraphrasing Complaints
*   **Scenario Context:** An upset customer calls complaining that their internet connection has been dropping multiple times a day.
*   **AI Customer Opening Message:** *"I am extremely frustrated! My internet keeps cutting out while I'm working from home."*
*   **Evaluation Focus:** Expressing verbal empathy, paraphrasing the customer's connectivity concern, and assuring support.
*   **Coaching Tip:** Validate their frustration, paraphrase the connectivity issue, and reassure them that you are ready to help.

### Submodule 3: Objection Handling (Service Fee Pushback)
*   **Scenario Context:** A customer is calling to complain about an unexpected $15 service charge on their account.
*   **AI Customer Opening Message:** *"Why was I charged an extra fifteen dollars this month? I want this removed immediately!"*
*   **Evaluation Focus:** Active listening, explaining the charge structure without being defensive, and offering alternative value options.
*   **Coaching Tip:** Acknowledge the objection without being defensive. Explain the charge clearly and offer alternative value or courtesy review.

### Submodule 4: Hold Procedure & Information Retrieval
*   **Scenario Context:** A customer wants to know if a specific router model is in stock at their local warehouse.
*   **AI Customer Opening Message:** *"Can you check if you have the Netgear RAX40 router in stock at the downtown warehouse?"*
*   **Evaluation Focus:** Asking for permission to hold, establishing a clear expectation of the hold duration, and thanking them on return.
*   **Coaching Tip:** Ask for permission politely before placing the customer on hold, set a clear timeline expectation, and thank them when returning.

### Submodule 5: Professional Call Closing
*   **Scenario Context:** The agent has successfully updated the customer's payment method and the customer is satisfied.
*   **AI Customer Opening Message:** *"Alright, thank you, that is all I needed today."*
*   **Evaluation Focus:** Checking for any additional issues, thanking the customer, stating company name, and ending with warmth.
*   **Coaching Tip:** Offer further assistance, thank them for calling, mention the company name, and close warm and professionally.

## 3. ADVANCED LEVEL: High-Stakes BPO Skills (Pitch & Pushback Roleplay)
Focused on persuasion, de-escalation, negotiation, compliance boundary setting, and callback management under high stakes.

### Submodule 1: De-escalation (VIP Customer Retention)
*   **Scenario Situation:** You are a senior BPO agent. Mr. Chen has been a client for 7 years and has been routed 3 times today without resolution. He is now threatening to cancel his contract worth $4,000/month. You must de-escalate, own the issue, and offer a concrete resolution.
*   **AI Customer Opening Challenge:** *"I have been transferred THREE times today and nobody has fixed my problem. I want to speak to your manager RIGHT NOW or I am cancelling everything."*
*   **AI Challenge Pushbacks (Turns 2 & 3):**
    1. *"Your manager is the only one who can solve this! I'm tired of hearing promises from agents who can't do anything."*
    2. *"If you can't guarantee a resolution by the end of today, I'm taking my business to your competitor."*
*   **Evaluation Rubric:** Empathy, Professional Ownership, Urgency Tone, De-escalation phrasing.

### Submodule 2: Negotiation (Refund Alternatives)
*   **Scenario Situation:** A customer demands a full cash refund of $350 for a subscription service they used for 6 months, citing minor service glitches. Our policy strictly prohibits cash refunds after 30 days. You must negotiate an alternative value (credits, upgrades, extensions).
*   **AI Customer Opening Challenge:** *"I want my full three hundred and fifty dollars back! Your service had too many glitches this past half year, and I'm not taking no for an answer."*
*   **AI Challenge Pushbacks (Turns 2 & 3):**
    1. *"A credit doesn't pay my bills. I paid in cash, and I want cash back."*
    2. *"Fine, but a fifty-dollar credit is insulting. You'll have to do much better than that if you want to settle this."*
*   **Evaluation Rubric:** Boundary adherence, negotiation flexibility, value-focused persuasion.

### Submodule 3: Persuasive Upselling (Budget-conscious Client)
*   **Scenario Situation:** Mrs. Davis is a satisfied customer on a basic plan. You notice her usage consistently hits the limit. You must persuade her to upgrade to the premium plan ($20 more per month) by highlighting value, despite her budget concerns.
*   **AI Customer Opening Challenge:** *"I'm happy with my current service, but I keep getting these alerts that I'm running out of data. I really can't afford to pay any more than I do now."*
*   **AI Challenge Pushbacks (Turns 2 & 3):**
    1. *"Twenty dollars a month is two hundred and forty dollars a year! That's just too expensive for a bit more speed."*
    2. *"If I upgrade, can you waive the fee for the first three months, or give me some guarantee that it's worth it?"*
*   **Evaluation Rubric:** Needs-based selling, objection reframing, value-proposition structure.

### Submodule 4: Escalation & Callback Management
*   **Scenario Situation:** The client is experiencing a critical server outage mid-call. It requires level-3 network engineering. You cannot fix it yourself. You must escalate the issue, set a realistic callback expectation, and keep the client calm.
*   **AI Customer Opening Challenge:** *"My company's database is completely offline and we are losing thousands of dollars every hour! Fix it right now!"*
*   **AI Challenge Pushbacks (Turns 2 & 3):**
    1. *"Why can't you just reboot the server from your side? I don't want to wait for a callback!"*
    2. *"If I don't get a call in thirty minutes, I will hold you personally responsible for our losses."*
*   **Evaluation Rubric:** Crisis tone, clear escalation process explanation, deadline negotiation, assurance.

### Submodule 5: Compliance Boundaries (Security Verification Bypass)
*   **Scenario Situation:** An urgent, stressed caller claims to be the account owner's spouse. They demand immediate password reset and address changes but cannot provide the PIN or answer security questions. You must hold the compliance boundary politely.
*   **AI Customer Opening Challenge:** *"Look, my husband is in the hospital and I need to access our account immediately to pay a medical bill. I don't know his PIN or security questions, just reset the password!"*
*   **AI Challenge Pushbacks (Turns 2 & 3):**
    1. *"This is an emergency! Are you really going to let a compliance rule prevent me from helping my family?"*
    2. *"Let me talk to your supervisor. They will understand this is a medical emergency and bypass this verification."*
*   **Evaluation Rubric:** Compliance policy boundary holding, polite but firm refusal phrasing, support guidance.
